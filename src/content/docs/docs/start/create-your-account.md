Signing up takes a couple of minutes, start to finish.

## 1. Sign up

Go to [app.thalermark.com/sign-up](https://app.thalermark.com/sign-up). Enter
your name, email, and a password.

## 2. Confirm your email

Check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the link. (Nothing there?
Check spam, or make sure you typed your address correctly.)

## 3. Tell us about your business

A short welcome walks you through the basics:

- **Your business name**: what goes on your invoices.
- **How your business is set up**: sole proprietor, LLC, partnership, S-corp,
  or C-corp. Pick whichever matches how you file taxes. Not sure? Most
  one-person businesses that never filed special paperwork are sole
  proprietors, and you can change this later without losing anything.
- **Your business address and time zone**: used on invoices, and so that
  "today" on your reports means today where you work.

## 4. You're in

You'll land on your **Position** screen: money in, money out, what you're
owed, what you owe. It's all zeros right now. The fastest way to fix that is
to send your first invoice or snap your first receipt.

:::tip[Working with a team?]
You can invite other people to your workspace later from Settings. They get
an email, click through, and see the same business you do.
:::
