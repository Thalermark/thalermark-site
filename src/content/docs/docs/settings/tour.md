Settings lives in the account menu (your avatar, top right). It's organized
into three groups. What your role can't manage simply isn't shown, so your
screen may be shorter than this list.

## Your account

- **Profile**: your name, email, and sign-in password.
- **Team**: who's in the workspace and what each person can do. Full guide:
  [Working with your team](/docs/use/team/).
- **Privacy**: the anonymous usage-data choice. What it means:
  [Telemetry, in plain words](/docs/trust/telemetry/).

## Your business

Everything here is per company, and needs an owner or admin.

- **Business**: the big one. Your address, phone, and email (with per-field
  switches for showing them on invoices and estimates), your logo, the
  reply-to address for outgoing email, and how the business is set up. It
  also holds four quiet settings that change your numbers; those get
  [their own page](/docs/settings/numbers/). Closing a business, and the
  new-EIN takeover flow, live here too:
  [Running more than one business](/docs/use/companies/).
- **Accounts**: every place your money sits (checking, savings, the cash
  box, a credit card), so payments and expenses can say where money moved.
  Context: [You and the business](/docs/use/owner-money/).
- **Tax**: your named sales-tax rates. Full page:
  [Sales tax rates](/docs/settings/sales-tax/).
- **Payments**: card payments via Stripe, plus the offline methods you
  accept. Full guide: [Accept card payments](/docs/use/card-payments/).
- **Email templates**: the wording of the emails your customers get. Full
  page: [The emails customers get](/docs/settings/emails/).
- **Reminders**: the automatic chasing schedule for unpaid invoices. Covered
  in [Record getting paid](/docs/use/get-paid/).
- **AI**: connect a provider and turn on the AI features. Full guide:
  [The AI in your books](/docs/use/ai/).

## Data

- **Import & export**: bring contacts and items in from CSV, download the
  everything-ZIP ([your data is yours](/docs/trust/privacy/)), and enter
  starting balances if you're arriving from another system.
- **Activity**: recent changes across the whole workspace, newest first,
  each expandable to see exactly what changed. The same History that every
  record shows, in one feed.
- **About**: the version you're running.
