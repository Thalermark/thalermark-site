The lawn business and the plow business. The LLC and the side hustle that
predates it. One Thalermark workspace can hold them all, each with its own
books, invoices, contacts, and reports, fully separate.

## Add a company

From the account menu (your avatar), **+ Add company** (owners and admins).
Two questions:

- **Business name.**
- **How's it set up?** *Just me (sole proprietor)*, *Just me, with an
  LLC*, *Me and a partner or two (partnership)*, or a corporation taxed as
  an *S-corp* or *C-corp*. Pick whichever matches how it files taxes; this
  is what makes the [tax worksheet](/docs/use/reports/) mirror the right
  form.

Address, time zone, logo, and the rest live in **Settings → Business**,
per company.

## Switching

The account menu lists every company with a ✓ on the active one; click
another to switch. Everyone on the team can switch, and everything you look
at and record belongs to the company you're switched into.

## When a business winds down

**Settings → Business → Close this business.** A closed company stops taking
new work but keeps everything readable and every report working, so you can
still file its final return. It stays in the switcher under "Show closed,"
and you can reopen it if it was a mistake.

:::note[Same business, new paperwork?]
If you've registered what's really a *new* business (a new tax ID from the
IRS, say the sole proprietorship became an LLC), Thalermark has a guided
flow for exactly that. Change the business type in **Settings → Business**
and confirm it's a new business: the new company takes over
your customers, prices, and balances from a date you choose, and the old one
closes cleanly with its records intact. (Answered "new EIN" by mistake? It
can be undone.)
:::
