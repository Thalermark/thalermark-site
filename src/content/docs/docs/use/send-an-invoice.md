## Create it

1. Open **Invoices** in the top bar and click **+ New invoice**.
2. Pick the **Contact**. Start typing a name; if they're not in your list
   yet, choose **+ Add "…" as a new contact** and give them a name and email
   right there. (The email is optional, but you'll need it to send by email.)
3. The **Number**, **Issued** date, and **Due** date are pre-filled, with due
   defaulting to 30 days out. Change anything you like.
4. Add **line items**: describe the work, set quantity and price. Typing in
   the description also searches [your items list](/docs/use/items/), so you
   can pick a saved item and the price, unit, and tax settings fill in.
5. Charging sales tax? Tick **Taxable** on the line and pick a rate. The tax
   total is always worked out from the lines; you never type it.
6. Click **Create invoice**.

The invoice is saved as a **draft**, and nothing has gone to the customer
yet. You land on the invoice's own page, which is where everything else
happens.

## Send it

Click **Send invoice**. Thalermark emails your customer a link to a clean,
branded copy of the invoice they can open on any device, with a **Pay**
button if you've [set up card payments](/docs/use/card-payments/) and your
other accepted payment methods listed.

Other options under the send button:

- **Send to a different email…** is a one-off override, without editing the
  contact.
- **Mark sent without email** is for invoices you hand over some other way.
  Either way you get a **Share link** you can text or message to anyone.

:::tip[The pre-send sanity check]
If the amount looks off (ten times what you usually bill this customer, or
less than what the job cost you), Thalermark says so right above the Send
button. It's a heads-up for dropped zeros, not a blocker: "Send anyway if
that's right."
:::

## After it's sent

The invoice page tells you what happened: when the customer opened it, and,
if the email bounced, failed, or was marked as spam, that it **never reached
them**, with plain advice on what to do next.

From here:

- [Record getting paid](/docs/use/get-paid/): full, partial, or deposits.
- [Fix a mistake](/docs/use/fix-an-invoice/): pull it back, correct it,
  resend.
- **Duplicate** reuses any invoice as the starting point for a new one.
- **Void** cancels it for good; the customer's link stops working.
