Whether it's your spouse doing the books, a crew member sending invoices, or
your CPA at tax time: invite them and pick a role. Everyone works in the
same workspace, and every change keeps its author in the history.

## Invite someone

**Settings → Team → Invite a teammate**: their email, a role, **Send
invite**. They get an email, click through, and they're in.

(Self-hosting without email set up? The invite still works. Thalermark
gives you the link to send them yourself.)

## The roles

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The workspace creator. Everything, including transferring ownership. Exactly one per workspace. |
| **Admin** | All day-to-day work plus settings and team management. |
| **Member** | The working role: invoices, estimates, contacts, expenses. Can view reports, can't touch settings. |
| **Accountant** | Built for "give my CPA access at tax time": expenses and categories, the tax and ledger exports, but not invoicing, settings, or your team. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only. Sees everything, changes nothing. |

Change anyone's role from the same page, any time. What a role can't do
simply isn't shown to them. No error mazes.

## Housekeeping

- **Make owner** hands the workspace to someone else. You become an admin,
  and only they can hand it back.
- **Remove** cuts someone's access immediately. Nothing they entered is
  deleted, and their name stays on the history of everything they did.
- Anyone but the owner can **leave** on their own.
