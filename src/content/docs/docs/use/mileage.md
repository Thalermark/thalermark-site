Business driving is one of the most under-claimed deductions there is.
**Mileage** (in the account menu) makes each trip a five-second habit: date,
miles, what for. Logged.

## Log a trip

One row: **Date**, **Miles**, **What for** ("Drove to the Miller place"),
and which **Vehicle**. The page shows today's IRS rate per mile, and the
year's running total sits right at the top, in miles *and* dollars.

Say what the trip was for every time. As the app puts it, that's what makes
the deduction stick if it's ever questioned. Regular route? The **Again**
button re-logs a past trip with today's date.

:::note[Mileage never moves money]
Logging a trip records a deduction. It doesn't touch your bank balance or
your money-out number. It shows up where it matters: your
[tax worksheet](/docs/use/reports/).
:::

## Your vehicles

Add each vehicle you drive for work (the F-150, the van). The IRS asks a few
questions about every work vehicle: when you first used it for work,
whether you also drive it personally, whether you have another car for
personal use. Thalermark collects the answers now so there's nothing to
reconstruct in April, and it nags gently if a vehicle is missing answers.

Sold the truck? **Retire** it. Logged trips keep their miles and the
deduction; it just leaves the picker for new trips.

Trips to a [job](/docs/use/jobs/) can be logged right from the job's page,
same log, prefilled purpose.
