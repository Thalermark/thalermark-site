Sometimes you float the business from your own pocket; sometimes you pay
yourself. Neither is business income or a business expense. It's money
moving between you and the business, and recording it wrong is one of the
classic small-business bookkeeping mistakes. Thalermark gives it its own
door so you can't fall into it.

Find **Investments & withdrawals** in the account menu.

## Record it

**+ Record** asks one question. **What happened?**

- **I put my own money in**: you covered something from personal funds, or
  seeded the business account.
- **I paid myself / took money out**: a draw, in plain clothes.

Then the amount, the date, which account it went into (or came out of), and
an optional note.

Your dashboard balances update; your **Money in / Money out**, profit, and
tax worksheet don't. Exactly as it should be. The list keeps a tidy record
of every investment and withdrawal, with the usual delete-and-restore
safety net.

## Starting balances

Switching to Thalermark mid-stream? **Starting balances** (linked from this
page, and from Settings → Import & export) is three questions: when you
started, money in the bank, money customers already owed you, and money you
already owed.

Coming from QuickBooks, Xero, or Wave with real history? The "I have my
previous balances" option accepts your full closing balances, even
imported straight from a CSV export, so your numbers and your tax
worksheet cover the whole year, not just the part you've spent here.

## Every place your money sits

The accounts you pick from (checking, savings, the cash box, a credit card)
live in **Settings → Accounts**. Add each one once, and every payment,
expense, and withdrawal can say where the money actually moved.
