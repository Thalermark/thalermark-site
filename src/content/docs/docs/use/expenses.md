Every tank of gas, bag of mulch, and parking fee: get it on the books and
it's there at tax time. Two ways in: type it, or snap the receipt and let the
AI do the typing.

## Log an expense

**Expenses → + New expense.**

1. **Vendor**: type the name. Pick a match from your contacts, or just
   leave it as typed; you can also add them as a new vendor on the spot.
2. **Amount** and **Date**.
3. **Category**: pick from your business's category list (Supplies, Fuel,
   Repairs & Maintenance…). Not sure? Hit **✨ Suggest** and the
   [AI](/docs/use/ai/) proposes one from what you typed. You still review
   and save.
4. **Paid from** only appears if you've added
   [more than one account](/docs/use/owner-money/) (checking, a credit card,
   the cash box), so the money comes out of the right place.

Click **Save expense**, done.

:::tip[Buying something that lasts?]
The form's first question is the one that matters at tax time: **"Will you
use this for years?"** A mower, a trailer, a truck: log those as a
[big purchase](/docs/use/big-purchases/) instead. The link is right at the
top of the form.
:::

## Attach the receipt

Open the expense and use the **Receipt** panel. JPEG, PNG, or PDF, up to
10 MB. On your phone, the file picker opens straight into the camera. The
image is kept with the expense, so "find the receipt" stops being a
shoebox problem.

## Let the AI read it

With a receipt attached, **Auto-fill from receipt** has the AI read the
image (merchant, total, date, and a suggested category) and pre-fill the
edit form, marked as pre-filled from your receipt for you to review.
Nothing is saved until you say so.

If a receipt comes in without a vendor matched, the expense is flagged
**Needs review** so it doesn't quietly rot. The list has a filter for
exactly these.

## Fixing and finding

- **Edit** and **Duplicate** work as you'd expect (Duplicate resets the date
  to today).
- **Delete** takes an expense off your books but keeps it behind **Show
  deleted**, where it can be restored. The receipt image is kept.
- The list filters by date, category, and vendor, and each expense keeps a
  full **History** of who changed what.
