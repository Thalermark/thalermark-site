**Contacts** is one list for everyone: the people you bill and the people
you buy from. You never have to file anyone as a "customer" or a "vendor".
Thalermark works that out from what you do. Bill someone, they count as a
customer; buy from them, they count as a vendor. Same person can be both.

## Adding people

Mostly, you won't. The fastest way to add a contact is
[while invoicing](/docs/use/send-an-invoice/) or logging an expense, right
in the picker. For the full form, **Contacts → + New contact**: name, email,
phone, address, notes.

Two conveniences worth knowing:

- **Address autocomplete**: type the street (include the city or ZIP) and
  pick a suggestion; the rest of the address fills itself in.
- **Dupe warnings**: type a name or email that looks like someone you
  already have, and Thalermark shows the match with an **Open** button
  before you create a twin.

## What a contact's page tells you

Open anyone you've billed and the page turns into a briefing:

- **Billed** in the last 12 months, **owed now**, and estimates won.
- **Payment reliability**: "Always pays on time (8 invoices)", or how often
  they pay late and by how many days. Worth a glance before you agree to
  net-30 again.
- **What you usually bill them**, and their billing by month.
- The full **History** of every change.

## Statements

The **Statement** button builds a clean summary of every invoice and payment
with a running balance, for the customer who asks "what do I owe you
again?" Email it directly, or **Print / save PDF**.

## Archiving

Contacts are never deleted. **Archive** hides someone from the pickers
while every invoice and expense that names them stays intact. **Restore**
brings them back.
