When you open Thalermark you land on **Where you stand**: one screen that
answers the question you actually have. *How is the business doing?*

## The four numbers

- **Money in**: what came in over the period you've picked.
- **Money out**: what went out.
- **Owed to you**: unpaid invoices, right now.
- **Owed by you**: unpaid bills, right now. Click it to see them.

The period switcher above them flips between **This month**, **Last 30
days**, and **Year to date**. The two "owed" numbers ignore it; they're
always right now.

## Right now

Below the money row, a strip of counts shows what needs attention, and each
one is a shortcut to the exact list:

- **Overdue**: invoices past their due date, "needs chasing."
- **Awaiting**: sent invoices not yet due.
- **Drafts**: invoices you haven't sent yet.
- **Open estimates**: quotes waiting on an answer.
- **Accepted**: estimates that said yes, ready to invoice.
- **Not delivered** appears only if an invoice email bounced or failed, so
  you find out from the dashboard, not from an awkward phone call.

Once you have a few months of history, a **Billed by month** chart appears
too.

## Unusual spending

If a month's spending jumps well above your usual, the dashboard says so in a
sentence, overall or for a single category. This comes straight from your
numbers, no AI involved, and it only appears when something actually looks
off.

## What to watch

With [AI connected](/docs/use/ai/), a **What to watch** section reads your
cash flow and offers a few short observations, each one grounded in a real
dollar figure from your books and tinted good, neutral, or warning. If AI
isn't set up, the section simply isn't there.

## Find anything fast

Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) anywhere in the app and start typing: a
customer's name, an invoice number, an amount, a word from a receipt memo.
Search covers invoices, estimates, contacts, expenses, bills, jobs, and
items.
