For work you bill on a rhythm (every Tuesday at the Chens', a monthly
retainer), set up a repeating invoice once and stop thinking about it.

## Set up a schedule

From **Invoices**, open the menu on the invoice button and choose **Set up a
repeating invoice** (or **Repeating invoices** to see the ones you have).

The schedule asks for:

- **Contact** and the **first invoice** date.
- **Frequency**: weekly, monthly, or yearly, plus **Repeat every** for
  things like every-other-week (set it to 2).
- **Payment terms**: how many days after each invoice goes out it's due.
- Optionally an **End date** or **Stop after N invoices**. Leave both blank
  and it runs until you say otherwise.
- The **line items**, exactly like a normal invoice. The totals card shows
  what each generated invoice will come to.

## What happens on the day

On schedule, Thalermark creates the invoice, numbers it, issues it, and
emails it to the customer. No draft waiting for you, no morning task. Each
generated invoice then behaves like any other: it can be
[paid](/docs/use/get-paid/), chased by reminders, even
[pulled back and fixed](/docs/use/fix-an-invoice/).

The schedule's page lists every invoice it has created, shows the **next
run**, and has a **Generate next now** button when you don't feel like
waiting.

:::tip[Pause, don't end]
**Pause** stops the schedule and **Resume** picks it back up. Use it for a
customer's vacation month. **End** is forever: an ended schedule can't be
restarted or changed, only replaced. The invoices it already created stay put
either way.
:::
