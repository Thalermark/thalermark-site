**Reports** (top bar) is a dozen cards, each answering one question. Most
take a time period (this month, this quarter, year to date, last year, or
any custom range), and every one exports to CSV.

## The everyday ones

- **Profit & loss**: revenue minus expenses, what you actually made.
- **Expenses by category**: where the money went, biggest first.
- **Who owes you**: unpaid invoices, grouped by how overdue they are, so
  you know who to chase.
- **Sales by contact**: your best customers by revenue.
- **Revenue over time**: the monthly trend.
- **What each job made**: billed minus tagged costs, per
  [job](/docs/use/jobs/).
- **Estimate win rate**: how many quotes turn into work. (Quotes that ran
  out without an answer are counted separately. Nobody said no, they said
  nothing.)
- **Top products**: best sellers from [your items list](/docs/use/items/).
- **Sales tax collected**: tax billed on invoices, ready to remit.
- **Balance sheet**: what the business owns and owes.

## The tax worksheet

The one that earns its keep in April. The worksheet mirrors **the actual
form your business files** (Schedule C for a sole proprietor, the
partnership or corporation forms otherwise), with your year laid out line by
line, ready to hand to whoever does your taxes. Pick the tax year, flip
between counting **when paid** and **when invoiced**, choose per-mile or
actual vehicle costs, and **Print / save PDF**.

Everything feeds it automatically: [expenses](/docs/use/expenses/),
[big purchases](/docs/use/big-purchases/) and their write-offs,
[mileage](/docs/use/mileage/), the lot.

:::note[Awareness, not advice]
Thalermark's tax numbers exist so April never blindsides you. They're not
financial or tax advice. The final word belongs to you and your tax
professional.
:::

## For your accountant

The **General ledger** card (visible to owners, admins, and accountants)
exports the complete detail behind your books in the format accountants and
tax software expect. You never need to open it, but the person who files
for you will be glad it's there. Better yet, invite them as an
[Accountant](/docs/use/team/) and let them pull it themselves.
