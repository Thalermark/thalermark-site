When a customer pays by card through their invoice link, Thalermark records
it for you. Everything else (cash, check, Venmo, Zelle) you record yourself,
from the invoice's page.

## Paid in full, in one tap

On a sent invoice, use **Mark paid**. The quick menu lets you pick the method
in one go (**Cash**, **Check**, **Venmo**, **Zelle**, or **Other**), or open
the panel to add a check number, the date, and which account the money was
**Deposited into**.

## Partial payments

Use **Record a payment** instead and enter the amount that actually arrived.
The invoice keeps honest score: *"$600.00 of $1,200.00 · $600.00 still
owed."* Record as many payments as it takes; the invoice flips to **paid** on
its own when the balance hits zero.

The same form handles **refunds**: switch the type to "Refund or credit."
And if you record something by mistake, every payment row has a **Remove**
that reverses it cleanly.

## Deposits before the work starts

A draft invoice has a **Received a deposit?** section. Enter what they gave
you and click **Record it**: the invoice is issued and the deposit is logged
in one step, and the customer's copy shows **Paid to date** and **Balance
due**. You can still email it to them whenever you like.

## Let reminders do the chasing

Turn on automatic reminders in **Settings → Reminders**: a gentle heads-up
before the due date, a nudge after, on the schedule you choose. Reminders
always quote what's *still owed* (a customer who paid a deposit is only
chased for the balance), and they stop the moment an invoice is paid in
full.

Got a customer who needs kid gloves? Every invoice has its own **Pause for
this invoice** switch, so you can keep reminders on for everyone else.
