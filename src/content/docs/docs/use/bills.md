A **bill** is something you owe a vendor and will pay later: the supplier
who invoices you monthly, the subcontractor you settle up with on Friday.
Record it when it arrives; mark it paid when you actually pay. In between,
it counts toward **Owed by you** on your dashboard, so the number never lies
to you.

Find Bills in the account menu (your avatar, top right).

## Record a bill

**Bills → + New bill**: the vendor, a category, the amount, the bill date
and the due date, plus the vendor's own invoice number as a **Reference**
if you want it.

:::tip[Paying off a credit card?]
Pick the card itself from the "Credit card statement" group in the category
list. As the form says: the things you bought on it were already counted
when you bought them, so the statement payment shouldn't count them twice.
:::

## Pay it

- **Mark paid** settles it in one go: method, date, and which account it
  was **Paid from**.
- **Record a payment** handles installments. The bill keeps score
  (*"$500.00 of $2,000.00 · $1,500.00 still owing"*) and closes itself when
  the balance reaches zero. Vendor refunds go in the same form.
- **Void** cancels a bill that shouldn't exist; what was still owed comes
  off your total.

## Who's waiting, and how long

The **Aging report** (top of the Bills list) buckets every open bill by how
overdue it is: current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days. Friday's "who do I
actually need to pay?" takes ten seconds.
